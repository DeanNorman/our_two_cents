# Phase 1: Discovery Mode
## Your Practical Guide — Dean & Abigail

**Duration:** 6–8 weeks (minimum 2 full calendar months of data)
**Goal:** Build the tracking habit. Learn where your money goes. Zero judgment.

---

## The One Rule

**Log everything. Judge nothing.**

You're scientists collecting data, not accountants balancing books. If Dean buys a R80 coffee, log it. If Abigail pays R12,000 rent, log it. No "should we have spent that?" — that comes later.

---

## How to Log a Spend

Open WhatsApp. Send one message to the bot. Done.

### Format

```
[category] [amount] [merchant (optional)]
```

### Examples

```
groceries 350 woolworths
dining 85 nandos
transport 450 shell
entertainment 200 netflix
utilities 1200 city power
housing 12000 rent
shopping 500 takealot
health 850 mediclinic
savings 8000 house fund
other 150 car wash
```

### Tips

- **Keep it simple.** `groceries 350` is enough. You don't need the merchant.
- **Don't overthink categories.** Coffee? Could be `dining` or `entertainment`. Pick one and be consistent — that matters more than being "correct."
- **Log within 24 hours** while you remember.
- **Round amounts** if you want. `groceries 350` is fine instead of `groceries 347.50`.
- **Both of you log from your own phones.** The bot tracks who sent it.

## Who Logs What

### Dean

- Your personal spending (allowance, lunches, petrol, coffee)
- Any shared purchases you make (groceries, takeaway, date nights)
- Cash withdrawals → log as `other [amount] cash`

### Abigail

- All bills and fixed costs (rent, utilities, insurance, medical aid)
- Your personal spending
- Any shared purchases you make
- Debit orders → log each one on the day it goes off

### Split Purchases

If Dean buys groceries for R600 but it's a household expense:
- Dean logs: `groceries 600 woolworths`
- That's it. Don't split it. The system tracks WHO spent it, so you'll see the full picture later.

## Your Weekly Ritual: The Sunday Recon

**When:** Sunday evening, 15 minutes
**Where:** Couch, with tea/wine
**Mood:** Curiosity, not anxiety

### What to Do

1. **Open the dashboard together** on a phone or laptop
2. **Look at the summary card** — total spent this month, transaction count, tracking streak
3. **Scroll through recent transactions** — "Oh yeah, that Woolworths trip was big"
4. **Glance at category bars** — which categories are largest?
5. **Talk about what you notice** — not what you should change, just what you see

### What to Say

✅ "Interesting, dining is our second biggest category"
✅ "We had a lot of transport costs this week"
✅ "We've tracked 12 days in a row, nice"
✅ "Oh I forgot to log that Clicks trip, let me do it now"

### What NOT to Say

❌ "We spent too much on X"
❌ "You shouldn't have bought that"
❌ "We need to cut back on Y"
❌ "This is stressful"

**If it feels stressful, you're doing it wrong.** This is observation only. Like checking the weather — you don't get mad at clouds.

## Your Monthly Check-In

At the end of each month (last Sunday), spend 30 minutes instead of 15.

### Month 1 Check-In

Look at the dashboard and answer together:

1. **Total spent this month:** R______
2. **Biggest category:** ____________
3. **Smallest category:** ____________
4. **How many transactions:** ______
5. **Days tracked out of total:** ____/____
6. **Anything surprising?** ____________

Write these down somewhere (notes app, paper, whatever). You'll compare with Month 2.

### Month 2 Check-In

Now you have two months. This is where it gets interesting:

1. **Month 1 total:** R______ | **Month 2 total:** R______
2. **Were they similar?** Or was one month unusual?
3. **Which categories stayed roughly the same?** Those are your "fixed" costs.
4. **Which categories changed?** Those are your "flexible" costs.
5. **Any patterns?** ("We always spend more on groceries in the first week")

**This is the data that feeds Phase 2.** The more consistently you track, the more useful Phase 2 becomes.

## Tracking Your Streak

The dashboard shows your tracking streak — consecutive days where at least one of you logged a spend. This is Phase 1's hero metric.

**Targets:**
- Week 1–2: Just get used to it. Forgetting is normal.
- Week 3–4: Aim for 5+ days per week.
- Month 2: Aim for 80%+ of days tracked.

**Don't stress about breaks.** If you miss a day, log yesterday's spends today. The streak resets but your data is still there.

## When to Move to Phase 2

You're ready when ALL of these are true:

- [ ] Tracked for 2 full calendar months
- [ ] 80%+ of days have at least one transaction logged
- [ ] Both of you are logging consistently (not just one person)
- [ ] You've done at least 6 Weekly Recons
- [ ] You've done the Month 1 and Month 2 check-ins
- [ ] You can roughly name your top 3 spending categories from memory
- [ ] It feels easy, not like a chore

**How to advance:** Click the phase badge on the dashboard sidebar → select Phase 2 → confirm.

If you're not sure, stay in Phase 1 for another month. There's no rush. Bad data from sloppy tracking is worse than spending an extra month building the habit.

## Troubleshooting

**"The bot didn't understand my message"**
→ Make sure you include a category keyword and a number. `groceries 350` works. `I bought food` doesn't.

**"I logged the wrong amount"**
→ Type `undo` to delete your last transaction, then re-log it.

**"I forgot to log yesterday's purchases"**
→ Log them today. The bot uses today's date, so your date will be slightly off — that's fine for Phase 1. Consistent logging matters more than perfect dates.

**"Which category should I use?"**
→ Pick the closest one and stick with it. Consistency > accuracy. If coffee is always `dining`, keep it as `dining`.

**"The dashboard shows no data"**
→ Make sure the backend server is running (`node server.js` in the backend folder). Check that your `.env` has the correct Google Sheets ID.

**"My streak reset"**
→ Streaks count consecutive days. If neither of you logged anything yesterday, it resets. That's okay — just start again.
